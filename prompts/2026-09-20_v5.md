## 2. Verified defect ledger (add as W-01 … W-14 in `research/audit_status.json`, severity in brackets)

**W-01 [BLOCKER] Minimum-size bids are silently rejected, so simulated quoting is ask-only on min-size-bound markets (e.g. BTC).**
Evidence: `src/sim/engine.py::_schedule_quote_update` requires `target_size × target_price ≥ venue.get_min_executable_clip() − 1e-6`, where `get_min_executable_clip = max(min_notional, min_order_size × current_mid)`. A bid is below mid, so 0.0001 BTC at 80,996 = $8.0996 fails against $8.1000 while the ask at 81,004.1 passes. Reproduced: BTC mid 81,000.05, tick 0.1, step/min size 0.0001, `FixedSpreadStrategy(spread_bps=1, clip_notional=10)` places only a SELL. Consistent with BTC paper rows being identical across strategies.
Fix: validate against the **venue's own rules using the order's own price**: `size ≥ min_order_size`, `size ≤ max_order_size`, `price × size ≥ min_notional`, size on step, price on tick. Remove the mid-based clip comparison. Also make strategy sizing round **up** to the minimum valid step when the requested clip rounds below it.
Acceptance: test that both bid and ask rest at minimum size in BTC-like, HYPE-like and ZEC-like specs; regression mutation MUT-18 "mid-based min-clip check".

**W-02 [BLOCKER] The walk-forward significance test is tautological.**
Evidence: `src/walk_forward.py::compute_two_sided_p_value` scores each fill as `(mid_at_fill − price)/mid` (BUY) or `(price − mid_at_fill)/mid` (SELL): spread captured at the instant of the fill. Any quote placed away from mid scores positive by construction; post-fill price movement (adverse selection), inventory, taker exit and funding are ignored. `evaluate_gates` also relaxes the fill gate to `≥ 100`, and `scripts/run_oos.py`, referenced by the pre-registration, does not exist.
Fix: Section 4.

**W-03 [MAJOR] The "Adaptive (Microprice/OFI lean)" strategy never receives the microprice/OFI signal.**
Evidence: `AdaptiveMicrostructureStrategy.generate_quotes(..., microprice_dev_bps=0.0)` defaults to zero and `SimEngine._evaluate_strategies_quoting` never passes it (no `microprice`/`ofi` reference exists in `src/sim/engine.py`). The OFI skew term is therefore always 0, so configs 3–4 in the pre-registration are just volatility-based spread plus inventory lean.
Fix: compute microprice deviation and trade-flow imbalance inside the engine from the book/trade stream (causal, no lookahead) and pass them to every strategy call, in live and replay identically. Acceptance: test that a nonzero imbalance changes the quotes; mutation MUT-19 "drop OFI argument". If you decide not to implement it, rename the strategy `Adaptive_VolInventory` everywhere and delete OFI claims. Decide before the freeze.

**W-04 [MAJOR] A +0.75 bps maker rebate is assumed but does not exist at this fee tier.**
Evidence: `scripts/run_pilot_analysis.py:255` (`MAKER_REBATE_BPS = 0.75`), `research/prereg_backtest.md` H1 ("−0.75 bps maker rebate"), `scripts/verify_report.py:55` (`CANONICAL_MAKER_REBATE_BPS = 0.75`).
